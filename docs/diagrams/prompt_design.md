Good System Prompt
       │
       ├── 1. ROLE
       │      Who are you?
       │
       ├── 2. OBJECTIVE
       │      What are you trying to accomplish?
       │
       ├── 3. RESPONSIBILITIES
       │      What should you do?
       │
       ├── 4. BOUNDARIES
       │      What should you NOT do?
       │
       ├── 5. PROCESS
       │      How should you approach the task?
       │
       ├── 6. OUTPUT CONTRACT
       │      What should you return?
       │
       └── 7. FAILURE BEHAVIOR
              What if you can't complete the task?
